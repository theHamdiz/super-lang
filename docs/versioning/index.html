Versioning and stability

The product, crates, CLI, installers, site, and release tag are 0.1.0. The language specification documents the Round 5 revision of Super 0.1.0; that revision is not a separate product version. The published benchmarks remain the frozen 0.1.0 measurement, taken on a single machine. Breaking syntax or stdlib changes may land until a future 1.0 stability promise. Releases follow vMAJOR.MINOR.PATCH tags; see CHANGELOG.md.
